## Instructions

1. Bring the water for the rice to a boil. Finely grate the ginger and mince the garlic. Heat half the sunflower oil in a covered saucepan over medium heat. Add the ginger and half the garlic; cook for 1–2 minutes.
2. Add the rice and boiling water. Crumble in the stock cube, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes. Remove from the heat and leave covered until serving.
3. Thinly slice the red onion into half-rings. Heat the remaining sunflower oil in a frying pan over high heat. Add the ground pork and onion; cook for about 3 minutes, breaking up the pork as it browns and cooking it through.
4. Finely chop the chili and herbs. Remove the pan from the heat and stir them into the pork. Zest the lime, cut it into wedges, and squeeze one wedge over the pork. Season with lime zest, salt, and pepper.
5. For the dipping sauce, mix the remaining garlic with the sugar, white wine vinegar, fish sauce, and water for the sauce.
6. Divide the rice and pork between plates. Serve with the dipping sauce and remaining lime wedge. The salad is omitted.

## Notes

- The source lists 10 ml of fish sauce per serving, while its instructions specify 1½ teaspoons. This recipe uses the listed ingredient amount.
