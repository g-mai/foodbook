## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Drain the sweetcorn and finely chop the red onion.
2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and half the sweetcorn for 1–2 minutes. Add the ground chicken and break it up as it cooks for 2–3 minutes. Stir in the passata and cook for another 2–4 minutes.
3. Fill the tortillas with the chicken mixture, fold them closed, and place them side by side in an oven dish. Sprinkle with cheddar and bake for 5–10 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, dice the tomato. Whisk the white balsamic vinegar with the extra-virgin olive oil, salt, and pepper. Just before serving, toss with the tomato, lettuce, and remaining sweetcorn.
5. Serve the enchiladas with the salad and sour cream.
