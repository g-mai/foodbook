## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour and dashi in a large bowl until smooth. Stir in the egg and grated nagaimo, then season with the salt and soy sauce. Mix in the cabbage, green onions, and pickled ginger.
2. Heat a little oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the noodles and water, cover for 1 minute, then loosen and stir-fry them. Season with salt and pepper, stir in the Tonkatsu sauce, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, and mirin, then set aside.
3. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat. Pour one quarter of the batter into a 20 cm circle. Top with half the noodles, then cover the noodles with another quarter of the batter and gently flatten. Lay 3 slices of pork belly on top.
4. Fry until the bottom is golden brown, about 5–7 minutes. Flip and cook until the pork is crispy, about 5–7 minutes more. Flip once more so the pork side faces up.
5. Spread okonomiyaki sauce and mayonnaise over the top, then sprinkle with aonori and bonito flakes. Serve hot and repeat with the remaining ingredients.
