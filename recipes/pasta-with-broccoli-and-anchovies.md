## Instructions

1. Trim the broccoli into florets, discarding any tough parts of the central stems, and rinse them under cold water.
2. Finely chop the garlic and thinly slice the chili. Warm a drizzle of olive oil in a pan, add the garlic and chili, then stir in the anchovies over low heat until they dissolve.
3. Blanch the broccoli florets in plenty of salted boiling water for about 5 minutes. Drain them into the pan, reserving the cooking water, and cook with a ladleful of that water.
4. Bring the broccoli water back to a boil and cook the linguine in it. Meanwhile, gently mash the broccoli with a fork.
5. Drain the pasta while al dente and toss it with the broccoli, adding a ladleful of pasta water to bring everything together. Finish with a drizzle of raw olive oil and serve hot.
