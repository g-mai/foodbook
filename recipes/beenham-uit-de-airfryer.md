## Instructions

1. Preheat the air fryer to 175 °C. Strip the thyme leaves from their stems and mix them with the mustard, sugar, vinegar, and olive oil. Season with pepper and, if desired, salt.
2. Remove the ham from its packaging, coat it on all sides with the marinade, and return it to its aluminium tray. Place the tray in the air fryer and cook for about 40 minutes, until the ham is cooked through.

## Notes

- The source lists a total preparation time of 45 minutes, including about 40 minutes in the air fryer.
- Equipment: an air fryer and the aluminium roasting tray supplied with the ham.
- Use uncooked Dutch beenham intended for roasting; the source does not use ready-to-eat cooked ham.
