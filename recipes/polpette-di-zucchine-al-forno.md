## Instructions

1. Wash the zucchini and trim off the ends. Coarsely grate them, then squeeze them well with your hands to remove their excess liquid. Put them in a bowl with the egg, stale bread crumb, finely chopped herbs, salt, pepper, and Parmesan.
2. Mix everything by hand until evenly combined. Shape the mixture into small patties and flatten them slightly between your palms. Arrange them on a lightly oiled, parchment-lined baking sheet.
3. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for about 25 minutes, turning the patties halfway through so they brown evenly. Serve hot.
