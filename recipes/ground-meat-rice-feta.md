## Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to the package instructions.
2. Finely chop the onion and cut the bell pepper into pieces.
3. Brown the ground meat in a pan, breaking it up as it cooks. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook for a few minutes.
4. Add the garlic, paprika powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
5. Add the diced tomatoes and let the sauce simmer gently for a few minutes.
6. Fold the cooked rice into the meat mixture and heat through.
7. Crumble the feta over the top just before serving.
