## Instructions

1. Boil 1.3 cm potato cubes in salted water for 15–20 minutes. Reserve 120 ml cooking water; drain and cover.
2. Quarter mushrooms; dice carrots and half the onion; mince half the thyme leaves. Brown beef in cooking oil for 4–6 minutes, breaking it up and seasoning. Transfer to paper towels; discard excess grease.
3. In the same pan, cook salted mushrooms in half the olive oil for 5 minutes. Add remaining oil, carrots, onion, and salt; cook 5–7 minutes.
4. Stir in 14 g butter, thyme, garlic powder, and flour for 1 minute, then tomato paste. Add water, concentrates, and beef; scrape the pan. Boil, then simmer 2–3 minutes. Season; transfer to a baking dish if needed.
5. Mash potatoes with sour cream, half the cheddar, remaining butter, and reserved water as needed. Season.
6. Spread mash over filling, leaving a 2.5 cm border; top with remaining cheddar.
7. Broil on high for 3–4 minutes, watching closely. Rest 5 minutes.
