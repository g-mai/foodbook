## Instructions

1. Peel and dice the carrot, roughly chop the onion, and dice the celery. Heat the olive oil in a wide, shallow pan and sauté the vegetables over high heat for about 1 minute.
2. Add the chicken skin-side down and lightly brown it on both sides. Season with salt and pepper, then add the garlic, rosemary, sage, and bay leaf.
3. Pour in the red wine and let it evaporate briefly. Add the tomatoes and a ladleful of hot water or stock. Cover and cook for about 20 minutes, adding a little more hot water if needed.
4. Halfway through cooking, remove the garlic and herbs and add the olives. Once the chicken is cooked through, take the pan off the heat and let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

## Notes

- For very large chicken pieces or drumsticks, the source recommends about 10 additional minutes of cooking. The listed 25-minute cooking time is the source's standard timing.
- The dish can be prepared a few hours ahead. Reheat it gently on the stovetop, adding a little hot water if needed.
