# Example content

Shared demonstration recipes and their images belong here, separate from personal cookbook content. This directory is outside Astro's page and asset entry points; examples are not published automatically.

The recipe schema and rendering are still planned. Until they are implemented, the proposed recipe format is documented in the root README. When usable examples are added, include their photos and attribution here, with instructions for copying them into a personal cookbook.

Personal recipes belong in `src/content/recipes/`, and their photos in `src/assets/recipes/`. Keep those directories empty in the public Foodbook repository, apart from their `.gitkeep` placeholders.
